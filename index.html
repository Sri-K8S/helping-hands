Hello,

I am from India, and this page is one of the hardest things I have ever written.

For the past few years, my life has been filled with challenges I never imagined I would face. I underwent multiple medical procedures and faced a long recovery period. Along with the physical recovery came emotional stress, financial pressure, and responsibilities that continued to grow.

During this difficult time, I also suffered losses in investments. Medical expenses, investment losses, and existing responsibilities eventually turned into a burden that now feels overwhelming. Today, my total outstanding debt is approximately $84,000 USD, making it increasingly difficult to regain financial stability.

I take full responsibility for my financial decisions. I do not blame anyone, and I am not looking for sympathy. What I am doing is facing my problems honestly and asking for help to rebuild.

Today, I continue to work, repay my loans, and meet every financial responsibility I can. Based on my current repayment capacity, it may take nearly 15–20 years to become debt-free. The hardest part is knowing that by the time I finally recover, I may have spent two decades simply returning to where I once was.

But I still believe tomorrow can be better than today. That belief is why I created this page.

My Journey Of Rebuilding

Every person's life has moments that completely change its direction.

Mine came through health challenges, financial setbacks, and decisions that did not work out as I had hoped.

The medical procedures tested my strength. The financial losses tested my patience. The debt continues to test my resilience every single day.

Even so, I wake up each morning determined to keep moving forward. I continue paying my EMIs. I continue working. I continue believing that responsibility means facing problems rather than running away from them.

There are days when the future feels overwhelming, but I remind myself that every repayment, every small step, and every act of kindness brings me closer to rebuilding my life.

I cannot change the past. I cannot undo the financial losses. What I can do is accept responsibility, learn from my mistakes, and continue rebuilding with honesty. That is exactly what I am trying to do.

"I cannot rewrite my past, but with your kindness, I can begin writing a better future."

Why I'm Asking for Help

Asking for help is not easy.

In fact, creating this page took me a long time because I always believed I should solve my problems on my own. I still believe that. That is why I continue repaying every loan and fulfilling every responsibility I have.

This fundraiser is not about avoiding responsibility. It is about reducing the burden enough that my family and I have a realistic chance to rebuild our future sooner rather than decades from now.

If enough kind people each contribute a small amount, together it can make a meaningful difference. Sometimes, many small acts of kindness become one life-changing opportunity.

Every Small Act Of Kindness Matters

A single contribution may not seem like much.

But when many compassionate people each choose to help a little, something remarkable can happen.

The price of a coffee. A meal. A movie ticket. Something small for one person can become hope for someone else.

Whether you choose to contribute $10, $25, $50, $100, $500, $1,000, or any amount that feels comfortable to you, please know that your generosity will never be taken for granted.

Every contribution represents more than financial support. It reminds me that kindness still exists. That strangers still care. That difficult chapters can still have hopeful endings.

"I cannot rewrite my past, but with your kindness, I can begin writing a better future."

"Your support does not erase my responsibility. It simply helps me carry it."

"Sometimes hope arrives through the kindness of people we have never met."
