My Story

I accept full responsibility for my financial decisions. In the pursuit of wealth, I lost approximately ₹70 lakhs through options trading. I am not blaming the market, brokers, or anyone else. The mistakes were mine, and I have learned painful lessons from them.

Today, I am committed to repaying my debts. Even with continuous EMI payments, becoming completely debt-free may take around 10 to 15 years. This is a long journey, and I am prepared for it.

What makes this journey difficult is that alongside repaying my loans, I also have the responsibility of supporting my family and managing essential household expenses. The weight of carrying both debt and family responsibilities has become increasingly challenging.

I am asking for voluntary support from anyone who wishes to help. This is not about escaping responsibility—it is about reducing the burden enough to keep my family stable while I work toward recovery.

"I am not asking anyone to solve my problems completely. I am only asking for a chance to rebuild my future, one step at a time."
