My Story: A Lesson in Responsibility & Hope

📉

The Mistake: Full Responsibility

I made serious decisions in options trading without fully understanding the risks. I lost approximately ₹70 lakhs. This was my choice, my mistake, and I own it completely. No excuses. No blame on the market or anyone else. I was wrong.

💰

The Reality: 10-15 Years of EMI

Even with consistent EMI payments, it will take me 10 to 15 years to repay everything. I'm not complaining—I'm accepting reality. This is the debt I created, and this is the path I must walk. At age 55, time is precious, but I'm committed.

👨‍👩‍👧

The Struggle: Balancing Family & Debt

While repaying loans, I also have family responsibilities—daily expenses, education, healthcare, food. The weight is heavy. I'm not seeking someone to carry it for me, but I'm asking if anyone can help ease the load so I can stay afloat.

🌱

The Ask: A Chance to Rebuild

This is not about escaping responsibility. This is about getting a fighting chance. Every contribution, no matter how small, reduces the immediate pressure and allows me to breathe. With your help, I can rebuild—not from scratch, but stronger than before.

Why This Matters at 55

At my age, losing 10-15 years to debt repayment alone means losing precious time with family, career growth, and building security for retirement. But more importantly, it means the difference between my family struggling and my family thriving. This is not about me—it's about the people I love.
