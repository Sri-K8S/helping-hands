One difficult decision changed my financial journey. But one mistake should not decide the rest of a person's life.

I made a serious mistake through options trading, resulting in a financial loss of approximately ₹70 lakhs. I take complete responsibility for my decision and the consequences that followed.

Today, I am working towards rebuilding my financial stability with discipline and patience. Based on my current repayment capacity, becoming completely debt-free may take around 15–20 years.

The most difficult part is not only the debt itself, but the amount of time required to recover. Spending the next 15–20 years only clearing this burden means reaching a stage around 50–55 years of age where I may have to begin rebuilding savings, financial security, and future plans from the beginning.

This journey has taught me important lessons about responsibility, risk, patience, and the value of financial stability. I am seeking a second chance to reduce this burden, rebuild my life, and move forward with the lessons I have learned.

My Journey Of Rebuilding

Life sometimes gives us lessons through experiences we never expected. MMy journey changed after a major financial mistake when options trading resulted in a loss of approximately around $84000 USD..

I accept responsibility for my decision. I do not blame the market, circumstances, or anyone else. This experience has changed the way I look at money, risk management, and long-term financial planning.

I continue to work towards repayment and financial recovery. However, with the current repayment timeline, it may take many years to overcome this burden completely.

The thought of spending such a long period only recovering from one mistake is challenging. After years of repayment, starting again with savings, investments, and financial stability can feel like rebuilding from zero.

But I believe that a difficult chapter does not have to become the entire story. With responsibility, determination, and support from kind people, a new beginning is possible.

"I am not asking anyone to erase my mistakes. I am asking for an opportunity to rebuild my future sooner."
