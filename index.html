My Journey Of Rebuilding

Life sometimes takes unexpected turns. Over the past few years, I have faced significant health challenges that required multiple medical procedures and a long recovery period.

During this difficult time, I also experienced losses in investments, which deepened my financial troubles. The combination of medical expenses, investment losses, and ongoing family responsibilities created a burden that has taken time to address.

I accept full responsibility for my financial decisions. I do not blame anyone or circumstances. This experience has changed how I approach money, planning, and life itself.

I continue to work hard and repay all my loans and monthly EMIs. Every month, I meet my financial obligations, but the timeline to become debt-free is long—approximately 15–20 years.

The reality is difficult to face. After years of repayment, I will be around 50–55 years old before I can truly begin rebuilding—savings, financial security, retirement plans—things many people establish much earlier in life. Starting over at that stage feels overwhelming, but not impossible.

But I believe a difficult chapter does not have to define an entire life. With hard work, responsibility, and support from compassionate people, a new beginning is possible. I am not asking anyone to solve my problems. I am asking for help to make the journey a little shorter.

"I cannot change the past, but I can continue working toward a better future."
